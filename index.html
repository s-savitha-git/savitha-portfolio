Selected Projects

Bakery

Rice Cracker

Gluten-Free Feasibility

01

Problem

Non-gluten dough lacked elasticity, causing sheeting failures and line breakage. A celiac-safe option was required.

Approach

  • Tested 4–5 rice flour varieties
  • Iterated 30–40 formulations
  • DOE on hydration and mixing time
  • Validated from lab to pilot scale
Outcome

Achieved a machine-feasible crispy cracker approved for pilot production, with reduced scrap rates.

Bakery

Millet Wafer

Structure & Binding

02

Problem

Millet flour caused weak binding and high breakage after baking, while standard wafer plates produced poor texture.

Approach

  • Optimized millet variety selection
  • Tuned starch interaction for binding
  • Adjusted batter rheology
  • Optimized process parameters
Outcome

Solved the binding issue and delivered a pilot-ready formula with high sensory acceptance.

Frozen

Frozen Patty

Cost & Stability

03

Problem

High formulation cost and poor freeze-thaw stability led to texture breakdown and machine losses.

Approach

  • Reformulated the binder system
  • Used clean-label starches
  • Optimized freezing rates
  • Validated hold times
Outcome

Fully commercialized; secured customer acceptance while significantly reducing COGS.
